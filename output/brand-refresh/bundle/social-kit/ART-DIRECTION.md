# Stackduck coming-soon art direction

Built-in Image Gen was used for both original illustrations. Exact logo lettering and campaign typography are composed separately using the established SVG and local fonts.

## Locked target

Match the established Stackduck palette. The user selected the ivory 3D duck on navy (image #2) as the primary app and profile identity. The navy duck on mint remains an alternate campaign illustration. Preserve the one-eye, right-facing duck, amber beak, curved wing, and two stack layers. Headlines are large Open Runde, the canvas is navy or mint, and website/handle/credit are explicit. No dashboard screenshots, invented metrics, feature availability dates, or stock gradients. A simplified companion is used only for tiny browser favicons. The app uses appropriately sized PNGs; the compatible logo.svg wraps the 3D PNG.

## Navy illustration prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Generate a premium 3D mascot illustration for Stackduck, a developer portfolio monitoring product. The attached logo is the exact identity reference: a simple ivory white duck silhouette facing RIGHT with a single dark navy round eye, short rounded amber beak, a deep curved navy smile/wing incision in its body, and two slim horizontal stack bars underneath (upper ivory, lower mint). Reinterpret the duck as a carefully sculpted matte ceramic 3D object, retaining these distinctive proportions and the friendly minimal identity. It sits on two softly rounded shallow horizontal ivory and mint stack slabs, with a little edge thickness and generous clean silhouette. Three-quarter view, beautifully soft studio light, subtle realistic ambient shadows, high end product render, not a toy photo, no feathers, no realistic animal detail, no extra eyes, no limbs. Square composition with the object filling the central 70%, full subject visible and very generous clean edge space for cropping. Perfectly solid dark navy background #151b31 and matching floor. Limited palette: ivory #f2f2f2 duck, amber #F5BA67 beak, mint #A8DCC4 lowest slab. No text, no letters, no floating UI, no watermark, no logo badge surround. This is a hero illustration to place beside clean marketing typography, NOT an entire flyer.

## Mint illustration prompt

Use case: ads-marketing. Create a premium 3D brand illustration for the Stackduck social campaign. Attached logo is identity reference only. Match its simple RIGHT-facing duck silhouette with one navy eye, short amber beak, and curved navy smile/wing cutout, but render it as a wonderful large matte NAVY ceramic sculpture #151b31. The navy duck stands over two separate low rounded stack bars, the upper navy and lower ivory. Keep the unique flat side silhouette with gently rounded three-dimensional edge depth, no feathers or limbs. Slight three-quarter view with a broad side face, not an actual rubber duck. Set the entire scene on a beautiful solid MINT #A8DCC4 backdrop and mint floor, subtle cast shadows, gentle studio illumination, tactile and beautifully crafted designer object, clean sophisticated indie developer brand. Center the complete sculpture in a square with 18 percent breathing room around it and no cropping. White ivory stack accent and amber beak only; no violet, no blue, no other objects. No text, no letters, no watermark, no UI windows. Hero art for a clean bold typographic coming-soon product campaign; art only, not full ad layout.
